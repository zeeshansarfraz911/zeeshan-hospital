Our Mission: Transform Healthcare Through Technology

We're building the digital infrastructure that connects every part of the healthcare ecosystem.

Our Story

Zeeshan Hospital was founded with a simple but powerful vision: make quality healthcare accessible to everyone through technology. We saw a fragmented system where patients, doctors, pharmacies, and labs operated in silos — leading to inefficiency, higher costs, and frustrated patients.

Today, we're proud to be Pakistan's fastest-growing digital healthcare platform, connecting 10,000+ patients with 50+ healthcare providers across 15+ specialties. Our platform handles everything from appointment booking to e-prescriptions, lab tests, and medicine delivery — all in one seamless experience.
