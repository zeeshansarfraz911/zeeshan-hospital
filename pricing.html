Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our plans

Can I switch plans later?▼
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. When upgrading, you get immediate access to new features. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
Is there a contract?▼
No long-term contracts. All plans are month-to-month. Enterprise plans can choose annual billing for additional savings.
What payment methods do you accept?▼
All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), bank transfers, and digital wallets. Enterprise customers can opt for invoice-based billing.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits?▼
Yes, we offer special pricing for non-profit healthcare organizations and government institutions. Contact our sales team for details.
