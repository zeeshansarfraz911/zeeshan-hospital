Everything You Need to Run Your Healthcare Practice

A complete suite of tools designed for patients, doctors, pharmacies, and labs — all in one platform.

🧍 For Patients

📅
Online Appointment Booking
Book appointments with specialists in seconds. Choose your preferred date, time, and doctor from available slots.
📼
Video Consultations
Connect with doctors via HD video calls from the comfort of your home. Recording and screen sharing supported.
✎
Digital Prescriptions
Receive e-prescriptions directly to your account. Printable, shareable, and integrated with partner pharmacies.
🔬
Lab Test Booking
Book lab tests online. Choose home collection or visit a partner lab. Get digital reports within hours.
🚚
Medicine Ordering
Order prescribed medicines and get them delivered to your doorstep. Real-time tracking and automatic refills.
📄
Medical Records Access
Secure cloud storage for all your health records. Access your complete medical history anytime, anywhere.

💉 For Doctors

👥
Patient Management
Comprehensive patient profiles with medical history, prescriptions, lab results, and appointment tracking.
✎
E-Prescriptions
Write digital prescriptions with medication suggestions, dosage calculators, and direct pharmacy integration.
📼
Video Consultations
Conduct secure HD video consultations with patients. Built-in note-taking and prescription tools.
📈
Practice Analytics
Real-time insights on patient volume, revenue, no-show rates, and clinical outcomes.
📅
Schedule Management
Manage your availability, block time slots, and handle appointment requests efficiently.
📝
Clinical Notes
SOAP notes, voice-to-text dictation, templated assessments, and secure storage for every patient encounter.

🏦 For Pharmacies

📝
Prescription Management
Receive digital prescriptions directly from doctors. Verify, process, and dispense with a single click.
📊
Inventory Tracking
Real-time inventory management with batch tracking, expiry alerts, and supplier management.
📦
Order Fulfillment
Streamlined order processing with pick-pack-ship workflow and delivery partner integration.

Low Stock Alerts
Automated alerts when stock falls below threshold. Never run out of essential medicines.
📈
Reporting
Detailed reports on sales, popular medications, expiry analysis, and revenue trends.

✅
Quality Control
Built-in QC tracking, proficiency testing management, and accreditation documentation support.
