Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Zeeshan Hospital

What is Zeeshan Hospital?▼
Zeeshan Hospital is a complete digital healthcare ecosystem that connects patients, doctors, pharmacies, and labs in one seamless platform. We provide video consultations, e-prescriptions, medicine delivery, lab test booking, and comprehensive practice management tools.
How do I get started?▼
Simply click the "Start Free Trial" button, create your account, and you'll have full access to all features for 14 days. No credit card required. Our onboarding team will guide you through the setup process.
Is my data secure?▼
Absolutely. We are HIPAA compliant and GDPR ready. All data is encrypted with 256-bit SSL, stored on AWS cloud infrastructure, and we maintain ISO 27001 certification for information security management.
Can I integrate with my existing EHR?▼
Yes, Zeeshan Hospital offers robust API access and pre-built integrations with major EHR systems including Epic, Cerner, and Meditech. Our integration team will ensure a smooth transition.
Do you offer training?▼
Yes, all plans include onboarding training for your team. Professional and Enterprise plans come with dedicated training sessions, comprehensive documentation, and video tutorials for every feature.
What payment methods do you accept?▼
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), bank transfers, and digital wallets. Enterprise customers can opt for invoice-based quarterly or annual billing.
Can I cancel anytime?▼
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees. Your data will remain accessible for 30 days after cancellation.
Do you have a mobile app?▼
Yes, Zeeshan Hospital offers native mobile apps for iOS and Android. Patients can book appointments, join video calls, and access records. Doctors can manage their practice on the go.
