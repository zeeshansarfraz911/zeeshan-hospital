What Is Telemedicine?

Telemedicine, also called virtual consultation or online doctor consultation, uses secure video technology to connect patients with doctors remotely. You can discuss symptoms, receive diagnosis, get prescriptions, and plan treatments — all without visiting the hospital.

Benefits of Online Medical Consultations

Save Time: No commuting, no waiting rooms. Average wait time for a Zeeshan Hospital video consultation is under 4 minutes.
Access Specialists: Connect with 120+ board-certified specialists across 35+ specialties.
Safe & Secure: HIPAA-compliant platform with end-to-end encryption.
Convenient Hours: Consultations available 7 days a week, 8 AM to 10 PM.

What Happens During a Video Consultation?

Your doctor will review your medical history, discuss your symptoms, ask relevant questions, and provide a diagnosis or treatment plan. If medication is needed, your prescription is sent digitally to your preferred pharmacy.
