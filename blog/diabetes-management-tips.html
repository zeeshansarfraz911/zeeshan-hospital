Diabetes Management in Pakistan: Expert Tips for Better Blood Sugar Control

By Dr. Ahmed Khan, MD, FACC · Cardiology Specialist · 9 min read

Pakistan has one of the highest diabetes rates in the world, with over 33 million adults living with the condition. At Zeeshan Hospital, our endocrinology and diabetes management team helps patients achieve better blood sugar control through personalized, evidence-based treatment plans.

Understanding Diabetes

Diabetes is a chronic condition where your body either doesn't produce enough insulin (Type 1) or doesn't use insulin effectively (Type 2). Both types require careful management to prevent complications affecting the heart, kidneys, eyes, and nerves.

Dietary Tips for Diabetes Management

Choose Low-GI Foods: Opt for whole grains, legumes, and vegetables over refined carbohydrates like white rice, white bread, and sugary drinks.
Portion Control: Use the plate method — half vegetables, quarter protein, quarter carbohydrates.
Limit Sugary Beverages: Avoid sodas, packaged juices, and sweetened teas. Drink water or unsweetened beverages.
Include Fiber: Vegetables, fruits, whole grains, and legumes help slow sugar absorption.
Eat Regular Meals: Skipping meals can cause dangerous blood sugar fluctuations.

Exercise and Physical Activity

Regular physical activity helps your body use insulin more effectively. Aim for 30 minutes of moderate exercise most days — brisk walking, cycling, or swimming. Always check your blood sugar before and after exercise, especially if you take insulin.

Medication and Monitoring

Take medications exactly as prescribed. Never adjust insulin or oral diabetes medications without consulting your doctor. Monitor your blood sugar regularly and keep a log to share with your healthcare team.

When to Consult a Specialist

Schedule a medical consultation if you experience: persistent high or low blood sugar readings, unexplained weight changes, vision changes, frequent infections, numbness in hands or feet, or difficulty managing your condition.
