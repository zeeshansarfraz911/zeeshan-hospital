Best Orthopedic Surgeon in Lahore: Complete Guide to Joint Replacement & Spine Surgery

By Dr. Hassan Raza, MD, PhD · Neurosurgery & Spine Specialist, Zeeshan Hospital · 10 min read

Orthopedic conditions affect millions of Pakistanis — from chronic back pain and arthritis to sports injuries and fractures. Finding the best orthopedic surgeon in Lahore can mean the difference between years of suffering and a return to active, pain-free living.

At Zeeshan Hospital, our orthopedic and spine surgery department combines world-class surgeons with robotic technology like the da Vinci and Mako systems. This guide covers everything you need to know about orthopedic care in Lahore.

Types of Orthopedic Surgeons

Joint Replacement Specialist: Performs hip replacement, knee replacement, and shoulder replacement using advanced robotic assistance for precise implant placement.
Spine Surgeon: Treats herniated discs, spinal stenosis, scoliosis, and fractures. Offers both minimally invasive and traditional surgical approaches.
Sports Medicine Specialist: Manages ACL tears, rotator cuff injuries, meniscus tears, and other sports-related injuries. Focuses on both surgical and non-surgical treatment.
Foot and Ankle Surgeon: Specializes in complex fractures, arthritis, and deformities of the foot and ankle.
Hand and Wrist Surgeon: Treats carpal tunnel syndrome, trigger finger, fractures, and tendon injuries.
Pediatric Orthopedist: Addresses growth-related conditions, congenital deformities, and fractures in children.

Common Orthopedic Conditions Treated in Lahore

Osteoarthritis (knee and hip), herniated discs and sciatica, rotator cuff tears, ACL and meniscus injuries, carpal tunnel syndrome, osteoporosis-related fractures, spinal stenosis, rheumatoid arthritis, and sports injuries. Many of these conditions can be managed non-surgically, but advanced cases often benefit from surgical intervention.

When to See an Orthopedic Surgeon

Schedule a consultation if you experience: persistent joint pain that limits daily activities, stiffness or swelling that does not improve with rest, difficulty walking, climbing stairs, or rising from a chair, back or neck pain radiating to arms or legs, numbness, tingling, or weakness in extremities, a sports injury that does not heal within 2 weeks, or worsening arthritis despite medication and physical therapy.
