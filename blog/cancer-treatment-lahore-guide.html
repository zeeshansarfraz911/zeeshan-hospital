A cancer diagnosis is life-changing, but advances in oncology mean more treatment options and better outcomes than ever before. At Zeeshan Hospital, our oncology department offers comprehensive cancer care — from diagnosis through treatment and survivorship — all in one integrated center.

This guide covers the cancer treatment options available in Lahore, what to expect during each phase of care, and how to access world-class oncology services close to home.

Types of Cancer Treatment Available in Lahore

Surgical Oncology: Removal of tumors and affected tissue by a specialized cancer surgeon. Often the first line of treatment for solid tumors. At Zeeshan Hospital, our surgeons use minimally invasive techniques including robotic-assisted surgery for faster recovery.
Chemotherapy: Powerful medications that kill cancer cells or stop them from growing. Can be given intravenously or orally. Our dedicated chemotherapy suite provides a comfortable, private environment for each patient.
Radiation Therapy: High-energy beams target and destroy cancer cells. Zeeshan Hospital offers IMRT (Intensity-Modulated Radiation Therapy) and IGRT (Image-Guided Radiation Therapy) for precise treatment.
Immunotherapy: Helps your immune system recognize and attack cancer cells. A groundbreaking treatment option for many types of cancer.
Targeted Therapy: Drugs that specifically target genetic mutations driving cancer growth, with fewer side effects than traditional chemotherapy.
Hormone Therapy: Used for hormone-sensitive cancers like breast and prostate cancer to block hormones that fuel cancer growth.

Common Cancers Treated in Lahore

Pakistan's cancer burden is significant, with breast cancer being the most common among women and oral cancer among men. Zeeshan Hospital's oncology team treats: breast cancer, lung cancer, colorectal cancer, oral and head & neck cancers, prostate cancer, lymphoma and leukemia, ovarian and uterine cancers, liver and pancreatic cancers, brain and spinal tumors, and childhood cancers.

Cancer Diagnosis and Staging

Accurate diagnosis is the foundation of effective cancer treatment. Our diagnostic services include: digital mammography and breast ultrasound, CT, MRI, and PET-CT imaging, image-guided biopsy, molecular and genomic testing, tumor marker analysis, and pathology consultation with second opinion. Our multidisciplinary tumor board reviews each case to create a personalized treatment plan.
